About

Research

I study the effects of technology and digital platforms on our discourse and collective wellbeing. My work deploys computational methods including machine learning, social network analysis, and multimodal large language models (LLMs) to study the influence of digital media and AI technologies on youth mental health, political communication, and disparities in social networks.

For my full list of publications, please see my Google Scholar.
