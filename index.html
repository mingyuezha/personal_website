About

I'm a Stamps Scholar at Dartmouth College studying Quantitative Social Science with minors in Chemistry and Neuroscience. Originally from Shanghai and raised in Connecticut, I bring together computational methods, social theory, and bench science to study how digital environments shape human behavior and well-being.

My research applies game theory, network analysis, and natural language processing to questions at the intersection of inequality and technology. Together with Professor Herbert Chang, I co-authored an award-winning paper examining gender disparities in online gaming communities, which received a Top Paper Award at the International Communication Association conference. I'm currently a Phi Beta Kappa awardee and a Teaching Assistant for QSS 20. I'm also conducting neuroscience research in the Jacobson Laboratory at the Geisel School of Medicine.

Looking ahead, I plan to pursue psychiatry — combining data-driven insights with clinical practice to provide inclusive, evidence-based mental health care.
